## 1. Why these rules exist

Paid challenges pay real money from a shared pool. Every step someone fakes takes money from
the people who walked. These rules explain how we check steps, when a payout is reviewed,
and what happens to money that is forfeited.

Our approach: **strict about money, generous about everything else.** Doubt can lower how
many steps count toward challenge money or pause a payout. It never erases an honest day
from your goals, streaks or XP, and no automated check closes an account on its own.

## 2. What counts as cheating

You must not:
1. make steps without walking or running yourself: shaking the phone, pendulums, fans,
   drills, "step machines", attaching the phone to a pet, a vehicle or another person;
2. give your phone to someone else to walk for you, or walk for someone else;
3. use more than one account, create accounts for others to control, or coordinate
   accounts to take more than one share of a prize;
4. use emulators, rooted or modified phones or apps, scripts, automation, or tamper with
   the app, its data or its network traffic;
5. record steps while riding in a vehicle (matatu, boda boda, car, train);
6. share or trade accounts, or use someone else's M-Pesa number to get around a hold.

Treadmills and indoor walking are allowed. Your steps count; they may count less toward
challenge money without GPS evidence (section 3).

## 3. How steps are verified

- **Every step you record counts for your goals, streaks and XP.**
- **Steps that count toward challenge money** need evidence that they came from walking:
  a supported wearable, a GPS **walk session** you start in the app, or motion patterns the
  phone's sensors recognise as walking. Steps without enough evidence may still show in
  your day but count less toward challenge money. The app shows each day's breakdown under
  *How were my steps counted?* with plain-language reasons.
- Daily limits apply to challenge credit (currently 60,000 credited steps a day) and to
  how fast steps can accumulate (a pace no person can sustain is not counted until time
  allows it).

## 4. Walk sessions

- You start a walk yourself with **Start a walk**. The app uses your location only while
  that walk is running and stops when you finish (see the Privacy Policy).
- A walk counts toward challenge money when its route, pace and motion look like walking or
  running. A walk that looks like a vehicle ride, uses mock locations, or runs on a device
  that fails integrity checks does not count toward challenge money (the steps still count
  for your goals).
- Walks end automatically after long inactivity.

## 5. Device and account rules

- Steps for your account sync from **one active phone** at a time. You can move to a new
  phone at most once every 24 hours.
- Each person may have **one** account. Family members sharing a phone or an M-Pesa number
  should tell support, so staff can mark them as a household.
- We may check the app and phone with Google Play Integrity or Apple App Attest.

## 6. Payout reviews

1. When a challenge ends, every qualifying winner's payout is calculated as usual.
2. A payout is **held for review** instead of being credited when, for example: the
   account's trust status is low, there are open high-severity fair-play flags in the
   challenge window, step days in the window were marked suspicious, a large payout comes
   with open flags, or the account is linked to another account in the same challenge (or,
   by the same phone, to an account already paid).
3. While held, the money is not in your wallet and can't be withdrawn or used for entries.
   You can't join new paid challenges until the review is finished.
4. A staff member reviews every held payout, normally **within 48 hours**, and either
   **releases** it (credited to your wallet like any payout) or **forfeits** it when the
   rules were broken. The app tells you the outcome.

## 7. Forfeited payouts

A forfeited payout is never kept as profit while honest players can receive it:

1. It is shared among the challenge's other **clear qualifiers** (paid instantly or
   released after review), in proportion to their original payouts.
2. If there are none, it is **refunded to the challenge's other entrants** in proportion to
   their entry fees (people whose own payout is held or forfeited, and banned or closed
   accounts, are left out).
3. Only if nobody is eligible is it recorded as platform revenue.

Amounts are split to the cent so the shares add up exactly to the forfeited amount.

## 8. Other consequences

Depending on how serious and repeated a breach is, staff may warn you, restrict challenge
entries, suspend step sync or close the account. A closed account's remaining wallet
balance that is not the proceeds of cheating is paid out to the account's registered
M-Pesa number **[confirm with lawyer]**.

## 9. Appeals

- You can appeal any hold, forfeit, restriction or closure through **Settings › Help &
  support** within **[14] days** of the decision. Explain what happened and add anything
  that helps (for example: you walked on a treadmill, you share a phone with family).
- A different staff member reviews the appeal and replies within **[7] days**.
- If we find we were wrong, we pay what you should have received. If the forfeited amount
  was already shared out, Step2Win pays you from its own funds.
- You can also complain to the Office of the Data Protection Commissioner about how your
  data was used, and use any other remedy the law gives you.

## 10. Changes

We may update these rules to keep challenges fair. Material changes are announced in the
app before they apply to challenges you join afterwards; challenges already running keep
the rules in force when they started.
