Delete your Step2Win account

You can permanently delete your account here or in the app (Settings → Delete account). This can't be undone.

What we delete

  • Your username, email, phone number and name
  • Your profile photo
  • Step, health and location (GPS) data
  • Device registrations and all signed-in sessions
  • Links to Google or Apple sign-in

What we keep (anonymised)

  • Wallet transactions, M-Pesa payments and withdrawals
  • Challenge entries and results
  • Fraud-prevention and audit records
  • Support tickets

These no longer show your name or contact details. We keep them to meet financial record-keeping and tax obligations.

Before you start

  • Withdraw any wallet balance to M-Pesa — we can't delete an account that still holds money.
  • Wait for any challenge you've joined to finish, and for pending withdrawals to be paid out.
